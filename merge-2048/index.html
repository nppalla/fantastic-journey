Join the tiles. Reach 2048.

How to play

Use the arrow keys to move all tiles. On a touch screen, swipe in a direction.

When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one tile with their sum.

Each move adds a new 2 or 4 tile to an empty cell.

Make a 2048 tile to win. You can keep going after you win.

The game ends when no move is possible.

Tips

  • Keep your largest tile in one corner.
  • Build a chain of decreasing tiles along one edge.
  • Avoid moving in the direction away from your corner.
  • Use Undo to recover from a bad move.
